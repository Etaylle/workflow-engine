# E-04  Domain `workflows`

```text
E-04  Domain `workflows`
Depends on:     E-03; E-05 for the team and steps shown on screen 8 (stub behind the published interface until then)
Status:         Proposed
```

**Scope.** Screens 4 Workflow, 5 Edit a task and 8 Ready to run: the BPMN-like business workflow generated through tools, shown with Svelte Flow, edited, validated and saved; the review and the immutable contract snapshot.

**Sources.** screens 4, 5, 8; `software-elements.md` 3.4, 3.5; BPMN level 1: Plan requirements and workflow, Send plan for review, Receive confirmed contract, Freeze contract; FR-WF, FR-CON, FR-AGT-07, FR-AGT-13.

**Risks.** Diagram editing is the largest frontend part; FR-AGT-13 (MCP door) is open issue OI-8.
